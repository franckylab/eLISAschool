`./docker/deploy.sh <local-dev|local-prod|cloud-dev|cloud-prod> [up|down|restart|rebuild|status|logs]` is the single entry point; it auto-detects host IP, generates secrets if `.env` contains `__AUTO_GENERATE__`, enforces min-length checks for `JWT_SECRET`/`DB_PASSWORD`/`ENCRYPTION_KEY` in prod modes, and prints access URLs. Backups are rotated automatically (daily >7d, weekly >28d, monthly >365d) and restored via `./docker/scripts/restore.sh <path/to/file.sql.gz>`.
