Express Router controllers, TypeORM entity decorators (`@Entity`, `@Column`, `@ManyToOne`, `@Index`), Zod for request validation (via `safeParse` in finances and `validateDto` from `@common/utils` in cantine/materiel), `node-cron` for scheduled finance jobs, and a centralized typed config pattern (`*DEFAULT_CONFIG` + `parseConfigValue`) exported from each module's `config/` folder.
