Each child is a self-contained Nest/Express module with its own controllers/dto/entities/services and an `index.ts` barrel; cross-child wiring happens at the application bootstrap where each module's `index.ts` registers its routes and TypeORM entities. The suite shares a common multi-tenant establishment scoping contract (every entity/service filters by `etablissement_id`) and a shared RBAC guard layer applied uniformly via controller decorators. Two explicit cross-module dependencies exist: `personnel_master` consumes `postes_fonctions` for poste/fonction references, and both `personnel_master` and `suivi_personnel` depend on `paie` for bulletin-paie and calcul-paie services, making paie the central financial anchor while recrutement feeds personnel onboarding.
