Scripts must be made executable once (`chmod +x scripts/*.sh`). The canonical bootstrap is `./scripts/deploy-complet.sh` (creates DB, runs all `backend/database/migrations/*.sql`, seeds, builds). Per-module rollout uses `./scripts/deploy-<module>.sh`. Development cycle: `./scripts/start-dev.sh [--backend|--frontend|--all]`, verify with `./scripts/verify-setup.sh`, stop with `./scripts/stop-dev.sh`. Many scripts assume either a `.env` with `DATABASE_URL` or hardcoded `DB_HOST=127.0.0.1:7002` with user `elisaschool_user`/password `elisaschool_password`.
