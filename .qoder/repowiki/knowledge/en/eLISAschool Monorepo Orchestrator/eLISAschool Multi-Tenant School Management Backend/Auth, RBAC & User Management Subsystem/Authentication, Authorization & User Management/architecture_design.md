Layered Express module organized by concern: `controllers/` expose HTTP routes (login, register, password reset, establishment switching) that delegate to `services/` for business logic; `middlewares/` provide request-scoped auth (`authMiddleware`, `optionalAuthMiddleware`) and permission checks (`requirePermission*`); `guards/` offer NestJS-style decorators (`permission.guard`). Entities under `entities/` are TypeORM models for users, roles, permissions, refresh tokens, audit logs, and multi-tenant `UtilisateurEtablissement` mappings. Cross-cutting concerns are isolated: `token.service.ts` handles JWT signing/validation and persistent refresh tokens, `audit.service.ts` centralizes audit logging, `blocage-auth.service.ts` implements the two-level lockout (per-user + per-IP), and `preference-*` services manage user/global preferences. Cron jobs in `cron-jobs/` and `cron-jobs.ts` schedule cleanup tasks via `node-cron`. Dependency direction is strictly controllers → services → entities/TypeORM repositories; middlewares/guards depend only on services and shared DTOs. The module re-exports everything through barrel files (`index.ts` per subdirectory) consumed by the app bootstrap.
