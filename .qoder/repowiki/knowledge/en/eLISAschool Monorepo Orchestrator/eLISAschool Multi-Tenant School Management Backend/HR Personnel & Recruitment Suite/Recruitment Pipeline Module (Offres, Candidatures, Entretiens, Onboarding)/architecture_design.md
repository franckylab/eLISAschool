Single-tenant TypeORM-based module split into four thin layers following a standard Nest-style layout: `entities/` declares four aggregates (`OffreEmploi`, `Candidature`, `Entretien`, `Onboarding`) plus their status/type enums; `dto/` exposes Create/Update/Query DTOs per aggregate; `services/recrutement.service.ts` is the sole business layer holding all CRUD, state transitions (e.g. `BROUILLON→PUBLIEE`, `RECUE→PRESLECTIONNEE→CONVOQUEE→RETENUE/REFUSEE`), cross-entity validation (offer must be published before accepting applications, duplicate email+offer guard), statistics queries, and audit logging via `@modules/auth`; `controllers/` exposes REST endpoints wired through a single controller file re-exported by `index.ts`. Cross-module dependencies are one-way: recrutement depends on `@modules/personnel/entities` (`MembrePersonnel`, `TypePersonnel`), `@modules/organisation/entities` (`UniteOrganisationnelle`, `Poste`), and `@modules/etablissement/entities` (`Etablissement`) for multi-tenancy scoping — every query is filtered by `etablissementId` and many entities cascade-delete with it. The service uses `AppDataSource.getRepository(...)` directly rather than constructor injection, and pagination is delegated to `@common/utils/pagination.util.paginateWithQueryBuilder`.
