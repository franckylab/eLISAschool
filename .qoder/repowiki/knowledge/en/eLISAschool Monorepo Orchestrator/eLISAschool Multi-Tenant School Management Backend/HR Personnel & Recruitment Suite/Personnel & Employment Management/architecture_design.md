Standard Nest-style leaf module split into four sibling folders (`entities/`, `dto/`, `services/`, `controllers/`) plus a barrel `index.ts` that re-exports everything. Each business concept (personnel, contrat, affectation, heure-cours, absence-personnel, evaluation, progression-programme, membre-fonction, type-contrat) gets one file per layer with matching names, wired together by the per-layer `index.ts`. Controllers are plain Express `Router`s (not Nest controllers) built per-file, instantiated once at module load, and mounted by the parent app; they delegate all logic to a corresponding service class. Services own TypeORM repositories via `AppDataSource.getRepository(...)`, enforce multi-tenancy through an `etablissementId` column present on every entity, and throw `AppError` for domain violations. Cross-module dependencies flow inward only: this module imports `TypePersonnel` and `Fonction` from `@modules/organisation/entities` and `Utilisateur`/`Etablissement` from their respective modules, but no other module imports back into personnel — establishing personnel as a consumer of organisation rather than a peer. The `membre-fonction` entity is the join table between `MembrePersonnel` and `Fonction`, carrying `estPrincipale`, date range, optional `ContratPersonnel` link and audit comment, which is the core of the refactored orthogonal TypePersonnel/Fonction model described in the coding intent.
