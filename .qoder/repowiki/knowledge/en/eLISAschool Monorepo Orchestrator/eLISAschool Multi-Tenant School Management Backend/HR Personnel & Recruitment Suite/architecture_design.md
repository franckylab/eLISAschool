Each child is a self-contained Nest module with its own entities/dto/services/controllers barrel-exported via an `index.ts`; cross-module wiring happens at the application root where these three modules are imported as peers rather than nested. The three modules share a common domain vocabulary (personnel, fonction, typePersonnel) and coordinate around the onboarding hand-off: `recrutement` produces an onboarded candidate who becomes a `Personnel` entity in `personnel`, while `suivi-personnel` consumes that same `Personnel` identity to attach incidents, evaluations and computed scores. A shared convention across all three is per-entity CRUD scaffolding (one controller + service + dto + entity pair per resource) plus a flat barrel export pattern (`export * from './entities' | './dto' | './services' | './controllers'`) so consumers import only the module index.
