Three sibling Nest/Express-style feature modules (`matieres`, `programmes`, `options`) each follow the same internal layering: `entities/` (TypeORM `@Entity` classes with UUID PKs, multi-tenant `etablissementId`, `CreateDateColumn`/`UpdateDateColumn`), `dto/` (class-validator input shapes), `services/` (stateless service class instantiated as a singleton via `export const xxxService = new XxxService()`), and `controllers/` (standalone Express `Router`s mounted under a module-level router exported from `index.ts`).

- `matieres/index.ts` re-exports sub-folders only; `programmes/index.ts` and `options/index.ts` additionally assemble an Express `Router` that mounts their controllers under `/chapitres`, `/correlation`, `/matieres`, `/` (programmes) and `/inscriptions` (options).
- Cross-module dependency direction is one-way: `options` depends on `matieres.entities.Matiere`; `matieres.service` lazily imports `programme-matiere.service` for `findProgrammesPedagogiquesByMatiere`. There is no reverse import.
- Multi-tenancy is enforced at every read/write by always scoping queries with `etablissementId` and attaching `@Index(['etablissementId'])` on entities.
- The `matieres` module owns three related aggregates: `GroupeMatiere`/`Matiere` (subject catalog), `MatiereNiveau` (subject-per-level grid of coefficient/bareme/volumeHoraire/credits/obligatoire), and `AffectationMatiere` + `ConfigurationMatiereClasse` (teacher assignment and per-class overrides). Effective values are resolved in `getConfigurationEffective` through the chain: ProgrammeMatiere → MatiereNiveau → defaults.
- `programmes` models the curriculum tree: `ProgrammePedagogique` (cycle/level/personalised) → `ProgrammeMatiere` (per-subject line item) → `ProgrammeChapitre` (chapter progression); `CorrelationController` exposes read-only correlation endpoints between programmes, progressions and evaluations.
