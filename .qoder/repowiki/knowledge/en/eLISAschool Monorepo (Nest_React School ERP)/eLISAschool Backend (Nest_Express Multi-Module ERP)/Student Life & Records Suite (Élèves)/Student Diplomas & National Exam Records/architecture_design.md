Standard three-layer Express + TypeORM layout under `src/modules/diplomes-eleves/`:
- `entities/diplome-eleve.entity.ts` — TypeORM `@Entity('diplomes_eleves')` model with UUID PK, `ManyToOne` relations to `Eleve` and `ExamenNational`, plus indexes on `eleveId`, `examenNationalId`, and their composite.
- `dto/diplome-eleve.dto.ts` — Zod schemas (`createDiplomeEleveSchema`, `updateDiplomeEleveSchema = partial(...)`, `queryDiplomesElevesSchema`) whose inferred types are re-exported as `CreateDiplomeEleveDto` / `UpdateDiplomeEleveDto` / `QueryDiplomesElevesDto`.
- `services/diplomes-eleves.service.ts` — stateless `DiplomesElevesService` class owning a `Repository<DiplomeEleve>` obtained via `AppDataSource.getRepository`; encapsulates business rules (duplicate-diploma guard in `create`, allowed-sort-field whitelist), delegates persistence to TypeORM, and throws `AppError` for 409/404 cases. Uses shared `paginateWithQueryBuilder` from `@common/utils/pagination.util`.
- `controllers/diplomes-eleves.controller.ts` — single Express `Router` exposing `/api/diplomes-eleves` REST: GET list (paginated), GET `/all` (dropdown-friendly), GET `/eleve/:eleveId`, GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE by id. Every route is guarded by `authMiddleware` plus `requirePermission('config:edit')` for mutations; request bodies are coerced/validated through `validateDto(schema, req.body)` before hitting the service.
- Module barrel `index.ts` re-exports entities, dto, services, controllers so consumers import from `@modules/diplomes-eleves`.
Dependency direction is strictly controller → service → TypeORM repository; cross-module imports go only outward to `@modules/auth/middlewares`, `@shared/enums/roles.enum`, `@database/data-source`, `@common/*`, and sibling modules `@modules/eleves/entities` and `@modules/examens-nationaux/entities`.
