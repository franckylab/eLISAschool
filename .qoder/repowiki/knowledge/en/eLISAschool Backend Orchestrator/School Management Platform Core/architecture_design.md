- **Modular Monolith Structure**: The platform is organized as a collection of domain-specific modules (e.g., `auth`, `finances`, `eleves`) exported via a central `index.ts` barrel file, enabling cohesive dependency management.
- **Shared Cross-Cutting Concerns**: Critical infrastructure modules like `auth` (authentication/RBAC), `configuration` (system settings/enums), and `notifications` (multi-channel alerts) are established as foundational dependencies consumed by business domains.
- **Domain Isolation & Integration**: Business modules (Academic, HR, Finance) maintain internal separation but integrate through shared entities and services, such as the `validation-workflow` engine for approval processes and `dashboard` for aggregated reporting.
- **Multi-Tenancy Enforcement**: Establishment-level isolation is enforced across modules via shared guards (`check-permission.middleware`, `groupe-access.guard`) and entity relationships linking resources to specific `etablissement` or `groupe-etablissements`.
