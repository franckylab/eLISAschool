The module follows a standard NestJS-like layered architecture within Express: `entities/` defines TypeORM entities (UniteOrganisationnelle, Poste, HierarchiePersonnel, EchelonStructurel, etc.) with UUID primary keys and multi-tenant `etablissementId` scoping; `dto/` contains Zod schemas for request validation; `services/` holds business logic split into focused services (organisation.service.ts, postes.service.ts, organigramme.pdf.service.ts, statistiques-optimisees.service.ts, template-organisation.service.ts); `controllers/` expose REST endpoints under `/api/organisation` mounted in `app.ts` with `authMiddleware`, `requirePermission`, and `filterByEtablissement()` guards. The barrel exports in each `index.ts` provide clean imports. Multi-tenancy is enforced at the controller layer via `getEtablissementId(req)` extracted from JWT, and Redis-backed caching is used for heavy operations like arborescence building.
