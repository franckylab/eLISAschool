Four sibling feature modules (`cantine`, `transport`, `parking`, `materiel`) each follow an identical vertical slice layout: `entities/` (TypeORM `@Entity` classes with UUID PKs), `dto/` (Zod schemas + `z.infer` types), `services/` (repository-backed business logic), `controllers/` (Express `Router` instances), plus a barrel `index.ts` re-exporting all four layers. Cross-module dependencies are one-way: entities import shared domain models from `@modules/auth/entities` (`Utilisateur`), `@modules/eleves/entities` (`Eleve`), and `@modules/etablissement/entities` (`Etablissement`); services additionally call into `@modules/responsables-eleves/services`, `@modules/validation-workflow/services`, `@modules/notifications/services`, and `@modules/configuration/utils/config.helper`. Multi-tenancy is enforced by an `etablissementId` FK on every entity, with indexes on `(etablissementId)` and composite keys. The transport service adds a process-scoped in-memory cache (`Map<string, {value;timestamp}>`) keyed by config parameter names with a 5-minute TTL, and uses `AppDataSource.createQueryRunner()` for transactional writes that combine persistence, workflow creation, and audit logging. Controllers delegate to singleton service instances created at module load time and rely on shared `authMiddleware` / `requirePermission` guards plus `validateDto` from `@common/utils`.
