- **Modular Monolith Structure**: The module is composed of six distinct sub-modules (`annees-scolaires`, `cycles`, `periodes`, `niveaux`, `matieres`, `programmes`), each following a strict layered architecture with `controllers`, `services`, `entities`, and `dto` directories.
- **Interface Boundaries**: Each sub-module exposes its functionality via Express Routers (e.g., `anneesScolairesController`, `programmesController`) which are aggregated in their respective `index.ts` files. The `programmes` module further aggregates `programmeChapitreController` and `correlationController`.
- **Dependency Direction**: Controllers depend on Services for business logic. Services interact with TypeORM Repositories for data persistence. Cross-module dependencies exist, such as `matieres` depending on `classes` and `annees-scolaires`, and `programmes` correlating data from `personnel` and `gamification` modules.
- **Validation Layer**: Input validation is handled via Zod schemas defined in `dto` files, enforced by a `validateDto` utility in controllers (or a local helper in `programmes`).
- **Workflow Integration**: Services like `AnneesScolairesService` and `PeriodesService` integrate with a `validationWorkflowService` to handle approval processes for critical state changes (e.g., closing a school year or period).
